HALVARD INDUSTRIAL CORP ANNOUNCES PRELIMINARY RESULTS OF ITS MODIFIED DUTCH AUCTION TENDER OFFER
Monday, October 5, 2026, 7:30 a.m. ET

Halvard Industrial Corp today announced the preliminary results of its modified "Dutch auction" tender offer, which
expired at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on Friday, October 2, 2026.

Based on the preliminary count by Stonemere Shareholder Services, LLC, the depositary, approximately 6.55 million
shares were properly tendered and not properly withdrawn at or below USD 32.50 per share, including shares tendered
by notice of guaranteed delivery. Halvard expects to accept for purchase 4,780,000 shares at a purchase price of
USD 32.50 per share. This includes 780,000 additional shares (2% of shares outstanding) that Halvard has elected to
purchase under its right to buy up to an additional 2% of outstanding shares without extending the offer.

Because the offer was oversubscribed, Halvard expects to purchase shares on a pro rata basis, other than odd lots,
with a **preliminary proration factor of approximately 72.9%**. The number of shares to be purchased and the purchase
price are preliminary and subject to change. Final results will be announced after the guaranteed delivery period
and the depositary's confirmation of proper delivery. Payment will be made promptly thereafter.
