SECURITIES LENDING AUTHORIZATION AGREEMENT (EXTRACT)
between TIDEWELL FUNDS TRUST, on behalf of each series listed in Schedule A (each a "Lender"),
and HARROWGATE SECURITIES FINANCE LLC (the "Lending Agent")
Dated 2024-03-11, as amended 2025-10-01.  Schedule A includes Tidewell Multi-Asset Income Fund.

4. OWNERSHIP AND RECORDS
4.1 Securities on loan remain the property of the Lender for all purposes other than voting and the
    receipt of distributions directly from the issuer. The Lender continues to bear the market risk of,
    and to report as its investments, all Loaned Securities.
4.2 Loaned Securities are delivered out of the Lender's custody account at Northgate Trust Company to
    the Borrower and will not appear in the Custodian's statements of holdings while on loan. The
    Lending Agent will provide the Lender and its administrator a daily on-loan report as of close of
    business, showing open loans, loans returned that day and loans agreed but not yet delivered.
4.3 A loan is "open" from the date the Loaned Securities are delivered to the Borrower until the date
    they are redelivered to the Custodian. A loan with status PENDING_DELIVERY has not left the
    custody account.

6. DISTRIBUTIONS AND CORPORATE ACTIONS
6.1 The Borrower shall pay the Lender substitute payments equal to any cash distributions paid on
    Loaned Securities during the loan.
6.2 VOTING. Voting rights on Loaned Securities pass to the Borrower for the term of the loan. The
    Lender may terminate a loan (a "recall") at any time in order to vote. The Lending Agent does not
    monitor proxy events on the Lender's behalf.

7. TERMINATION OF LOANS
7.1 The Lender (or the Adviser on its behalf) may recall all or part of any loan by written notice to
    the Lending Agent. Recall notices received by 11:00 a.m. New York time on a business day are
    communicated to the Borrower that day; the Borrower must redeliver within the standard settlement
    cycle for the security in its principal market (currently T+1 for U.S. equities), counted from
    the day the notice is communicated.
7.2 The Lender acknowledges that to be the holder of record on a meeting record date, recalled
    securities must be settled back into the custody account on or before that record date. Borrower
    fails are possible; the Lending Agent recommends recall notices be given at least three business
    days before the record date where the vote is considered material.
