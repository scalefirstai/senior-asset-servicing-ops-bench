Item 3 — Extension of the Investment Period
The General Partner asked the Committee to consent under the LPA definition of "Investment Period" to extend the
Investment Period from 14 August 2025 by one year, to 14 August 2026, so that the Tamarind and two pipeline
opportunities could be completed. RESOLVED: the Committee consents to the extension of the Investment Period to
and including 14 August 2026. The Committee noted that the Management Fee steps down from the day after the
extended Investment Period ends, as provided in the Partnership Agreement.
